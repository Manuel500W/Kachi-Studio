Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What this covers

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use kachistudio.site — mainly, when you fill out the contact form or message us on WhatsApp. Kachi Studio is a small, independent web development practice based in Nigeria; this isn't a large company with a data-processing department, and this policy is written to reflect that plainly.

What we collect

When you use the contact form, we collect what you type in: your name, an email address or phone number, the type of project you're asking about, and your message. We don't use analytics trackers, advertising cookies, or any hidden tracking scripts on this site — there is currently nothing else collected automatically beyond standard web server logs (like your general location and browser type) that any hosting provider keeps.

How we use it

Solely to reply to you about the project or question you reached out with. Your contact form submission is sent to our email and, where relevant, followed up on WhatsApp. We don't sell, rent, or share your information with third parties, and we don't add you to a mailing list without asking first.

WhatsApp and email

If you contact us by WhatsApp or email directly, that conversation is subject to WhatsApp's and your email provider's own privacy practices in addition to this policy. We keep those conversations only as long as needed to complete the project or respond to your enquiry.

How long we keep it

Contact form submissions and related project conversations are kept for as long as needed to respond to you and, if we work together, for the life of the project and a reasonable period after for record-keeping. You can ask us to delete your information at any time — see below.

Your rights

You can ask us, at any time, what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email kachistudio@outlook.com and we'll handle it directly — there's no automated system to navigate.

Changes to this policy

If how we handle information changes meaningfully, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: kachistudio@outlook.com or WhatsApp.
